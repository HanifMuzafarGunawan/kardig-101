# Kardig Wallet `v1.0.0.0`

Kardig Wallet is a lightweight, privacy-first open-source digital wallet designed to declutter your physical pockets. It serves as a minimalist alternative to mainstream digital wallets, allowing you to easily duplicate and digitize your physical cards and parking tickets.

## Why Kardig Wallet?

Most modern digital wallets rely heavily on cloud synchronization, mandatory user accounts, and continuous internet access, raising significant privacy and tracking concerns. Kardig Wallet addresses this by operating completely offline. 

Every piece of data is stored locally on your device, ensuring that you maintain absolute ownership, sovereignty, and control over your personal information without risking external data exposures or server breaches.

## Key Features

*   **QR-Based Card Duplication**
    Digitize any physical membership, loyalty, or identification card containing a standard QR code by simply scanning it. Your cards are stored organized and ready for use at any time.
*   **Parking Ticket Management**
    A newly introduced utility in this version that allows you to scan and save physical parking receipts or gate tickets, eliminating the risk of losing or misplacing them.
*   **100% Offline & Secure**
    The application runs entirely on local loops. With no cloud connections, remote API handshakes, or external data transmissions, your sensitive information is perfectly safe from online breaches.
*   **Clean and Minimalist UI**
    An intuitive, clutter-free user interface designed for quick navigation and seamless accessibility when you need your credentials on the go.
*   **Lightweight Performance**
    Built with efficiency in mind, the application consumes minimal system resources and memory, ensuring smooth background operation without slowing down your device.
